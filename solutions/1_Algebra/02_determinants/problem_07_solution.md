Exercise 7 Solution

Given a 3 by 3 matrix A with determinant of A = -4:

1) Determinant of 2A

For a 3 by 3 matrix, multiplying every entry by 2 multiplies the determinant by 2^3:

determinant of 2A = 2^3 * (-4)
                  = 8 * (-4)
                  = -32

2) Determinant of the transpose of A

Transposing a matrix does not change its determinant:

determinant of the transpose of A = -4

3) Determinant of A squared

The determinant of a product is the product of the determinants:

determinant of A squared = (-4) * (-4)
                         = 16

4) Determinant of -A

For a 3 by 3 matrix, multiplying by -1 multiplies the determinant by (-1)^3:

determinant of -A = (-1)^3 * (-4)
                  = 4

Conclusion

determinant of 2A = -32
determinant of the transpose of A = -4
determinant of A squared = 16
determinant of -A = 4
