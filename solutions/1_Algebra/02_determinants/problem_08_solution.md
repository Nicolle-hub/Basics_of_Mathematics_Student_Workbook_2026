Exercise 8 Solution

Given determinant of A = 3 and determinant of B = -5.

The determinant of a product is the product of the determinants:

determinant of AB = 3 * (-5) = -15
determinant of BA = (-5) * 3 = -15

Although AB and BA are not generally equal as matrices, their determinants are equal because multiplication of numbers is commutative.

Since the determinant of A is not zero, A inverse exists:

determinant of A inverse = 1 / 3

Therefore:

determinant of (A inverse * B) = (1 / 3) * (-5)
                               = -5 / 3

Conclusion

determinant of AB = -15
determinant of BA = -15
determinant of (A inverse * B) = -5 / 3
