Exercise 15 Solution

Given invertible 2 by 2 matrices A and B:

determinant of A = -2
determinant of B = 3

We need the determinant of (B inverse * transpose of A * B).

The determinant of a product is the product of its determinants:

determinant of (B inverse * transpose of A * B)
= determinant of B inverse * determinant of transpose of A * determinant of B

The determinant of an inverse is the reciprocal of the determinant:

determinant of B inverse = 1 / 3

Transposing a matrix does not change its determinant:

determinant of transpose of A = determinant of A = -2

Substitute these values:

determinant of (B inverse * transpose of A * B)
= (1 / 3) * (-2) * 3
= -2

The factors involving B cancel because (1 / 3) * 3 = 1.

Answer: The determinant is -2.
