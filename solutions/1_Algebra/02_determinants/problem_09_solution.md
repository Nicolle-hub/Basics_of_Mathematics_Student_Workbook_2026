Exercise 9 Solution

The vectors are:

u =
(
  3
  1
)

v =
(
  1
  4
)

Place the vectors as columns of a matrix:

(
  3  1
  1  4
)

The determinant is:

3 * 4 - 1 * 1 = 11

The area of the parallelogram is the absolute value of the determinant, so the area is 11 square units.

Reverse the vector order:

(
  1  3
  4  1
)

The determinant is:

1 * 1 - 3 * 4 = -11

The determinant changes sign, but the area is its absolute value:

|-11| = 11 square units

Therefore, reversing the vector order changes the sign but not the area.
