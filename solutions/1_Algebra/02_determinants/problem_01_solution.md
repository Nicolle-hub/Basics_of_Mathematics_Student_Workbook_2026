Exercise 1 Solution

For a 2 by 2 matrix, multiply the top-left entry by the bottom-right entry, then subtract the product of the other two entries.

First matrix:

(
  3  -2
  5   4
)

determinant = 3 * 4 - (-2) * 5
            = 12 + 10
            = 22

The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is not zero.

Second matrix:

(
  1   7
  2  14
)

determinant = 1 * 14 - 7 * 2
            = 14 - 14
            = 0

The second matrix is not invertible because its determinant is zero.

Conclusion

A square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is not zero.
